A LITTLE ABOUT ME

I've taken the scenic route into software.

I've been fascinated by computers since I was eight years old, when I spent far too much time tinkering with an Acorn Electron.

Somehow, it took me rather a long time to turn that fascination into a career. I've worked in marketing, the food industry and, for more than a decade, run my own yoga business.

Eventually I came back to where I'd started. I began studying full-stack software development in 2021, graduated in 2022, and haven't looked back.

HOW I THINK ABOUT SOFTWARE

I like solving the awkward problems.

The interesting projects are rarely the ones where everything already works perfectly.

They're the ones where someone says, "This takes us three hours every Friday", or "We're entering this information in three different places", or "Our software doesn't quite do what we need."

That's the stuff I enjoy. Understanding how something actually works, finding the unnecessary complexity, and building a better way of doing it.

THE OTHER SIDE OF MY WORK

Software isn't really about software.

It's about people.

I've run Great Malvern Yoga for more than a decade, which has taught me a great deal about communication, listening, organisation and understanding what people actually need — rather than what you assume they need.

Those skills are just as important when I'm working on a software project. The best solution in the world isn't much use if the people using it don't understand it, enjoy using it, or can't see why it makes their lives easier.

That's something I care about a lot.

ANOTHER PART OF WHAT I DO

A specialist side project.

I'm also a co-founder of The Integration Station, where I work specifically on software integrations and automation.

TIS gives me the opportunity to dive deeply into integration problems, particularly within the Zoho ecosystem, while Great Malvern Software gives me the freedom to work across a much broader range of software and web development projects.
